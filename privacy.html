Transplant Central LLC (“Transplant Central,” “we,” “us” or “our”) makes software for transplant centers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and share personal information when you:

  • visit transplantcentralhq.com (the “Site”);
  • contact us by email or through a form on the Site; or
  • receive text messages that we send on behalf of your transplant center (the “SMS Program”).

1. Health information we handle for transplant centers

Transplant centers use our software to support patient care. When we handle protected health information (“PHI”) for a center, we act as that center’s business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”). We use and disclose PHI only as our business associate agreement with the center and HIPAA allow.

Your transplant center’s Notice of Privacy Practices explains how the center uses health information and what rights you have. To use those rights, for example to see or correct your health records, contact your transplant center. If you send that kind of request to us, we will forward it to your center.

If this policy and a business associate agreement say different things about PHI, the business associate agreement controls.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you email us or fill in a form, we collect your name, work email address, organization, job title, phone number and anything you write in your message. Please do not send patient information through the Site or by email.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, our hosting provider’s servers record standard log data, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the date and time of your visit. [If you add an analytics tool, name it here.]
  • SMS Program information. Your transplant center gives us your name, mobile phone number, preferred language and care schedule so that we can send check-ins. We also keep a record of your consent, the messages we send and your replies.

3. How we use information

  • To answer your questions and requests, including requests for a pilot or a demo.
  • To run, protect and improve the Site.
  • To send SMS Program messages for your transplant center, process your replies and give them to your care team.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-out requests.
  • To meet legal duties and enforce our terms.

Automated tools, including AI language models that run in our secured cloud environment, help read and sort SMS replies. Replies that may be urgent, or that the tools cannot read with confidence, go to a person.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use SMS Program information for advertising.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only in these ways:

  • Service providers. Companies that host the Site and our software, deliver email and text messages, and give us security services. They may use the information only to provide services to us. Each service provider that handles PHI signs a business associate agreement with us.
  • Your transplant center. For the SMS Program, we give your replies and check-in results to the transplant center that enrolled you.
  • Legal reasons. When the law requires it, for example to answer a subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of patients, the public or Transplant Central.
  • Business transfers. If we take part in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may move to the new owner. This policy continues to protect it, and PHI stays under the business associate agreements in place.

5. Text messages and mobile information

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services and the transplant center for which we send the messages.

We use your mobile number only to send the SMS Program messages that your transplant center asked us to send. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages. Reply HELP for help. The SMS Terms in our Terms of Use give the full program details.

6. Cookies and Do Not Track

The Site does not use advertising cookies. [If you add analytics cookies, describe them here and explain how to turn them off.]

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. We do not track visitors across other websites, so the Site does not change how it works when it receives this signal.

7. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and contact information for as long as we need it for the reason you gave it to us, for our business records and to meet legal duties. Then we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

We keep SMS Program information for as long as our agreement with your transplant center and the center’s records policy require.

8. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards that fit the sensitivity of the information. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure. Standard text messages are not encrypted. Your transplant center tells you this before you join the SMS Program.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information that you gave us through the Site or by email. Send your request to george@transplantcentralhq.com. We will answer within a reasonable time and as the law requires.
  • You can stop SMS Program messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message.
  • For health information that your transplant center holds, contact the center.
  • Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you more rights. Where those laws apply to us, we will honor those rights.

10. Children

The Site is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. The SMS Program is for adults 18 and older. If you think that a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Before a material change takes effect, we will post a clear notice on the Site.
