The problem

Many lost organs follow missed medicines or labs.

Recipients must take transplant medicines on time, every day, for the life of the graft. Coordinators often learn about a missed dose late, at a clinic visit or after rounds of phone tag.

30–40%

of kidney transplants fail within 10 years.1

22%

of kidney recipients did not take medicines as prescribed (median across studies).2

36%

of graft losses were linked to earlier nonadherence (median).2

~7×

higher odds of graft failure for recipients who were nonadherent.2

How it works

What changes for your coordinators

The text helper does the routine asking. Your team triages the replies that need a person.

01 · Medicines

Daily dose check

Today

A missed dose comes to light at the next visit, or when a drug level comes back low.

With Transplant Central

Each recipient gets a short text at dose time and replies with a number. A missed dose goes on the worklist with the reason.

02 · Labs

Lab windows

Today

A missed blood draw shows up when no result arrives. Then the phone calls start.

With Transplant Central

A reminder goes out before the window opens. If no result comes in, the text helper asks what happened.

03 · Language

The patient's own language

Today

Follow-up calls wait until an interpreter is free.

With Transplant Central

Messages go out in the patient's language. A medical translator reviews every message before your center approves it.

04 · Safety

Urgent replies

Today

A worrying message can sit in a voicemail box overnight.

With Transplant Central

A reply that might mean danger goes to your on-call clinician at once. If nobody answers, the backup contact gets the alert.

05 · Follow-up

One worklist

Today

Follow-up lives in spreadsheets, inboxes and sticky notes.

With Transplant Central

Coordinators work from one list with urgent flags at the top. Each flag shows the patient's own words and the full history.

06 · Chart

Epic record

Planned after pilot launch

Today

Staff type adherence notes into the chart by hand, when they have time.

With Transplant Central

Each finished check-in files to the patient's Epic chart as discrete data, once your Epic team approves the link.

Safety and privacy

Rules we build by

Our users are immunosuppressed transplant recipients. These rules come before speed.

No medical advice

The text helper never tells a patient to start, stop or change a medicine. It sends every clinical question to a nurse.

A person for every urgent reply

When the system is not sure a reply is safe, it treats the reply as urgent. Every urgent path has a tested backup.

Your clinical content

Your medical director approves every question, urgent word list and patient message before it goes live.

Agreements before data

No patient data moves until we sign a business associate agreement with your center and with each vendor that touches the data.

Patients choose

Patients agree to texts in their own language first. They can reply STOP at any time.

Invented patients in testing

We build and test with synthetic patients only. Real patient data stays in the production system.
